About Me

Thomas Barr is the author of several short stories, a few poems, and Billing Ablutions, a dystopian realist novel for which he is actively seeking representation. He is based in New York City.

Join Newsletter

I send periodic fiction to subscribers by email newsletter once per month, along with updates on publications.

You may be wondering: Why not write a blog on one of the many platforms that exist? The short answer is: I want a reader-writer relationship that is as direct as possible. That means that when you subscribe, you'll get words sent directly to your inbox once a month. Nothing more, nothing less.
